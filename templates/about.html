About Project Aatmanirbhar

Who We Are

Project Aatmanirbhar is a community-driven initiative dedicated to empowering rural communities in the Yamuna Valley of Uttarakhand. The project works closely with local families, women entrepreneurs, and village communities to create sustainable livelihood opportunities while preserving the region's rich cultural and environmental heritage.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build self-reliant Himalayan communities where economic growth, environmental conservation, and cultural preservation go hand in hand. We believe that sustainable development begins at the grassroots level and directly benefits local families.

Women's Empowerment

A key focus of Project Aatmanirbhar is empowering women through skill development, entrepreneurship opportunities, and active participation in local economic activities. By supporting women-led initiatives, we aim to strengthen households and communities throughout the region.

Sustainable Tourism & Homestays

Through community-based homestays, visitors can experience authentic Himalayan village life while directly supporting local families. These homestays promote responsible tourism, generate additional income for rural households, and encourage the preservation of local traditions and hospitality.

Himalayan Herbs & Local Livelihoods

The project promotes the cultivation, value addition, and marketing of Himalayan herbs and nutraceutical products. By connecting local producers with broader markets, we help create sustainable income opportunities while showcasing the region's rich biodiversity.

Digital Inclusion

Project Aatmanirbhar works to bridge the digital gap in remote villages by introducing technology, digital literacy, and online opportunities. This enables rural communities to access information, education, services, and markets that contribute to long-term development.

Preserving Culture & Heritage

The Himalayan region is home to unique traditions, folklore, architecture, cuisine, and community values. Our initiative encourages visitors and residents alike to celebrate and preserve these cultural treasures for future generations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create sustainable livelihoods, strengthen rural economies, empower women, support eco-tourism, and connect Himalayan communities with new opportunities while maintaining harmony with nature and local traditions.
