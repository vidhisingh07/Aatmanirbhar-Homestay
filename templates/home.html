About Project Aatmanirbhar

Project Aatmanirbhar is a community-driven initiative working across the Yamuna Valley of Uttarakhand to create sustainable livelihoods and strengthen rural communities.

Through village homestays, women-led enterprises, eco-tourism, digital inclusion, and the promotion of Himalayan herbs and nutraceutical products, the project empowers local families while preserving the region's cultural and environmental heritage.

Visitors experience authentic Himalayan hospitality while directly supporting local communities, traditional knowledge, and sustainable development in mountain villages.
